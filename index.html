About

I am an Informatics student specializing in biomedical and health information systems, with experience designing scalable data platforms, coordinating cross-functional stakeholders, and supporting research and policy work through AI-assisted workflows.

My work focuses on building information systems that are structured, transparent, and maintainable over time. I enjoy translating complex requirements—technical, legal, and organizational—into practical tools that support collaboration and informed decision-making.

Selected Projects

HazingInfo.org — National Data Platform

HazingInfo.org is a public-facing information platform documenting hazing incidents across the United States. I supported the design and ongoing development of the platform’s underlying data systems, focusing on data quality, scalability, and long-term maintainability.

My work included defining data standards, structuring over 5,000 incident records across all 50 states, and implementing AI-assisted data collection and cleaning pipelines. I also coordinated with legal experts, academic researchers, and technical collaborators to align requirements and development milestones.

Role: Project Assistant · Tools: Airtable, Softr, AI-assisted workflows

Global Burden of Disease — AI-Assisted Systematic Reviews

As part of a research team supporting the Global Burden of Disease project, I evaluated AI-assisted screening tools used in large-scale systematic literature reviews. The goal was to assess how automation could improve efficiency while maintaining methodological rigor.

I contributed to comparative evaluations of screening performance, documentation of research workflows, and reproducible information management practices used to support synthesis and compliance.

Role: Project Contributor · Tools: DistillerSR, AI screening tools

Cooperative Software Development — Lead Teaching Assistant

I served as a Lead Teaching Assistant for a cooperative software development course emphasizing Agile methods and team-based delivery. In this role, I supported both instructional staff and student teams throughout the development lifecycle.

I coordinated teaching assistants, facilitated sprint planning and retrospectives across more than ten teams, and coached students on scope definition, prioritization, and collaboration practices.

Focus: Agile execution · Team coordination · Technical mentorship

One Child One Computer — Founder & Project Lead

I founded and led a nonprofit initiative aimed at reducing the digital divide for Turkish high school students. The project focused on providing access to technology while maintaining accountability and transparency in distribution.

Over three years, I raised $30,000, coordinated international logistics, managed volunteer teams, and supported the distribution of more than 200 laptops to underserved schools.

Focus: Leadership · Operations · Impact measurement
