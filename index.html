HazingInfo.org — National Hazing Incident Database

Goals of the Project

Create a centralized, searchable database to document hazing incidents across the United States in order to increase transparency and support research, journalism, and advocacy efforts.

My Role

Project Assistant responsible for data architecture, system maintenance, and coordination between technical, legal, and academic stakeholders.

Problem / Opportunity

Hazing incidents were difficult to track due to fragmented reporting and inconsistent data standards, limiting public understanding and analysis.

Process & Solution

I defined data standards and information architecture, structured over 5,000 incident records using Airtable, and implemented AI-assisted data collection and cleaning workflows. I collaborated closely with legal experts to ensure responsible data representation and iterated on the system based on stakeholder feedback.

Outcome & Next Steps

The platform is actively used by researchers, journalists, and advocacy organizations. Future work includes expanding data fields and improving public-facing visualizations.

Takeaways

This project strengthened my skills in information architecture, stakeholder coordination, and building systems designed for long-term use and accountability.

Global Burden of Disease — AI-Assisted Systematic Review Screening

Goals of the Project

Improve the efficiency and accuracy of large-scale systematic literature reviews through the evaluation of AI-assisted screening tools.

My Role

Project Contributor supporting evaluation, documentation, and research workflow development.

Problem / Opportunity

Manual screening for systematic reviews is time-intensive and resource heavy, creating an opportunity for responsible automation.

Process & Solution

I evaluated AI screening tools within DistillerSR, compared performance against human reviewers, and documented reproducible workflows to support transparency and research compliance.

Outcome & Next Steps

Screening speed and accuracy improved by approximately 30%. Future work includes refining ranking thresholds and expanding evaluation across additional review topics.

Takeaways

I learned how to critically evaluate AI tools in research settings and balance efficiency gains with methodological rigor.
