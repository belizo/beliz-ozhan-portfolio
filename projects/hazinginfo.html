HazingInfo.org

HazingInfo.org is a national transparency and accountability platform that centralizes institutional hazing policies, reporting mechanisms, and documented hazing incidents across U.S. colleges and universities.

Database Architecture & Core Data Model

At the core of HazingInfo.org is a relational database designed to normalize, validate, and connect multiple sources of hazing-related data across institutions, states, and time periods.

Each institution is evaluated using five primary transparency indicators: hazing policy availability, incident transparency reporting, reporting forms, designated university contacts, and hazing hotlines.

What We Do

HazingInfo.org improves safety and accountability in higher education by collecting, structuring, and publishing hazing-related data in a standardized, public-facing format.

Search & Discovery

Users can search colleges and universities by name or state to quickly understand each institution’s hazing prevention and transparency practices.

Institution Detail Pages

Institution pages consolidate hazing policies, reporting mechanisms, incident disclosures, and supporting documentation into a single, easy-to-navigate view.

My responsibilities included designing and maintaining the database schema, standardizing transparency indicators, validating incoming data, and coordinating updates across technical and non-technical stakeholders.

Outcomes & Takeaways

HazingInfo.org is now a live national resource used by journalists, researchers, advocacy organizations, and the public to better understand institutional hazing practices.

This project reinforced the importance of ethical data design, transparency, and cross-disciplinary collaboration in public-interest technology.
