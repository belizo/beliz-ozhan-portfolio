Project Overview

HazingInfo.org is a national transparency platform that structures data on college hazing incidents. My role focused on the data architecture and system design used to standardize institutional reporting.

TL;DR

Built the underlying data system for a national hazing transparency platform, transforming inconsistent institutional disclosures into structured, comparable data.

My Role

I worked as a Student Assistant on a multidisciplinary team and led database architecture decisions, ensuring data collection aligned with how information would be surfaced to users.

Problem

Universities publish hazing data inconsistently, making cross-institution comparison nearly impossible.

Solution

I designed a structured data system and documentation process that standardized how information is collected, verified, and stored across institutions.

Impact

  • Enabled cross-institution comparison for the first time
  • Improved data consistency across thousands of records
  • Reduced onboarding time for new contributors
